Our working standards.

Our policies support responsible, lawful and professional delivery across our business and security assignments.

Modern Slavery Statement

VSG does not tolerate slavery, servitude, forced or compulsory labour or human trafficking. We expect lawful and ethical conduct from our workforce and suppliers and will act on concerns raised through appropriate channels.

Corporate Social Responsibility

We aim to operate responsibly in the communities and environments in which we work. This includes fair treatment, professional conduct, responsible use of resources and consideration of the social and environmental impact of our activities.

Data Protection

Personal information is handled for legitimate business purposes and in line with applicable UK data protection requirements. Access to personal information should be limited to those who need it for an authorised purpose.

Equality, Diversity and Inclusion

We support fair treatment and equal opportunity. Employment and service decisions should be based on legitimate requirements and not unlawful discrimination.

Health and Safety

We expect security assignments to be planned and carried out with appropriate regard to health and safety. Site-specific risks, procedures and reporting arrangements should be communicated to personnel undertaking the work.
