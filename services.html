Professional security services.

From ongoing guarding contracts to temporary cover, VSG provides security personnel around the needs of each assignment.

Manned Guarding

Uniformed security officers for commercial premises, offices, industrial sites, construction environments and managed buildings.

  • Access control and visitor management
  • Internal and external patrols
  • Incident and occurrence reporting
  • Opening and closing duties where agreed

Door Supervision

Licensed door supervisors for venues and premises where customer safety, entry control and a professional front-of-house presence are required.

  • Entry and queue management
  • Conflict awareness and de-escalation
  • Venue policy support
  • Incident response and reporting

Event Security

Planned security support for events and temporary operations, with staffing discussed against the venue, audience and operating plan.

  • Access points and perimeter support
  • Guest and attendee management
  • Back-of-house security
  • Response and escalation arrangements

Corporate & Reception Security

A professional security presence for offices, reception areas and corporate environments where service and presentation are important.

  • Visitor sign-in support
  • Contractor access
  • Reception security
  • Site procedure compliance

Retail Security

Visible guarding support for retail environments, tailored to store procedures and customer-facing operations.

  • Visible deterrence

  • Incident recording
  • Staff support within agreed procedures

Temporary & Additional Cover
