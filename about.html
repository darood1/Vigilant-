Who we are

Security delivered with consistency and accountability.

Vigilant Guarding Solutions Ltd provides guarding and security support to organisations that need a dependable on-site presence.

We understand that a security officer often becomes part of the daily operation of a site. That means punctuality, presentation, communication and accurate reporting are just as important as vigilance.

How we work

Before cover begins, we establish the purpose of the assignment, site arrangements, key contacts and expected duties. Officers are then briefed against those requirements.

Our standards

We expect professional behaviour, appropriate presentation, confidentiality and clear incident reporting. Site instructions remain central to the way each assignment is delivered.

Client communication

We keep communication practical. Clients know who to contact, how issues are escalated and what information should be recorded during an assignment.

Flexible support

Requirements can change. We can discuss ongoing contracts, temporary cover, additional shifts and security support for particular events or operational periods.
